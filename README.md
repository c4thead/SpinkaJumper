Is a fork of an another jumper game adapted to my likings, ZERO VibeCoded

# Lore
Spinka's Delta Team experienced a huge failure and had to flee the scene.
You are the only one spinka alive from Delta and now running away from shrimps that are trying to take you down.
The run takes days and nights that eventually you might lose your mind and find yourself in the HELL!
You will find a gun on your way and if you run further, there's gonna be a golden one.
